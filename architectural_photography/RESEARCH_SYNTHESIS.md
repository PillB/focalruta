## Architectural-photography transfer cards
### Iwan Baan — INHABIT / CONTEXT
People and how they make/use spaces; architecture anchors a larger contextual story.

### Fernando Guerra — DECISIVE ARCHITECTURAL MOMENT
Architecture changes with light/hour; people/life can complete the image. Convert into activity windows, waiting and `STAY/MOVE/RETURN`.
- https://www.archdaily.com/45742/ad-photographers-fernando-guerra
- https://www.domusweb.it/en/architecture/gallery/2019/02/07/interview-with-architectural-photographer-ferando-guerra.html

### Ezra Stoller — DOCUMENT / UNDERSTAND
Careful vantage and light; line/form/texture convey three-dimensional architectural experience.
- https://www.ezrastoller.com/bio

### Hélène Binet — LIGHT / MATERIAL / MEMORY
Patient embodied understanding through light/material/time/memory; detail can imply the whole.
- https://www.scopionetwork.com/blog/hlne-binet-on-light-memory-and-the-experience-of-architecture
- https://www.theguardian.com/artanddesign/2021/oct/10/helene-binet-photographer-light-lines-royal-academy-interview

### Lucien Hervé — REDUCTION / SHADOW-LIGHT GEOMETRY
Near-abstraction, detail-as-whole, rigorous shadow/light tension.
- https://www.culture.gouv.fr/thematiques/architecture/actualites-de-l-architecture/Lucien-Herve-Geometrie-de-la-lumiere-au-Chateau-de-Tours

### Julius Shulman — DESIGNED COMPOSITION / LIFESTYLE
Dynamic composition/light timing and people can communicate spatial promise. Historical staging is a compositional lesson, not permission to falsify present reality.
- https://www.sfmoma.org/artist/Julius_Shulman/
- https://www.aaa.si.edu/collections/interviews/oral-history-interview-julius-shulman-11964

### Candida Höfer — MEANINGFUL ABSENCE
Empty institutional spaces can reveal structure, proportion, material, light and embedded social frameworks. Do not force people into every scene.
- https://konradfischergalerie.de/artists/85-candida-hofer/overview/

### Bas Princen / Gabriele Basilico lineage — URBAN SYSTEMS
Photography as critical reading of built environment, repetition/variation and social conditions.
- https://www.cca.qc.ca/en/articles/91475/lateral-gaze

### Leonardo Finotti — LATIN AMERICAN PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE

9. Work A→B→C→D→E.
